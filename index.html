Meet Conny
How it helps

The practical things, kept together.

Conny Cooper is designed to make everyday treatment organisation easier to understand and revisit.

  • Keep information together

    Organise medication and treatment details in one place.

  • See what is coming up

    View upcoming medication schedules and planned activities.

  • Receive your reminders

    Use reminders configured around your treatment schedule.

  • Record medication activity

    Keep a simple record of medication activity over time.

  • Review treatment information

    Look back at the information and activity you have recorded.

A more human experience

Treatment organisation does not have to feel cold.

Conny is being designed as a warm, approachable presence around practical treatment tasks—clear enough to be useful, and friendly enough to feel at home in everyday life.

Approved character artwork and product imagery will join Conny here as the app gets closer to release.

Trust and safety

Support for organising—not medical advice.

Conny Cooper helps users organise treatment information. It does not provide medical advice and does not replace a doctor, pharmacist or other qualified healthcare professional.

For medical emergencies, contact your local emergency services.
